> **Executive summary.** In Ecuador, a **legally binding electronic signature** must be issued with a certificate from a **Certificate Information Entity (ECI) accredited by ARCOTEL** (the Telecommunications Regulatory Agency). As of 2026, there are **17+ accredited ECIs**, both public and private. This page lists each one with the certificate format offered (`.p12`, USB token, HSM, cloud certificate), typical validity (1-2 years), reference price, and the official link to start the process. Data is sourced from ARCOTEL's public registry and each ECI's official site.

## What is an ECI and why does it matter?

A **Certificate Information Entity (ECI)** is the legal entity authorized to issue digital certificates with legal backing in Ecuador. Accreditation is granted by **ARCOTEL** under the **Electronic Commerce, Signatures and Data Messages Act (Law 2002-67)**. Without a certificate from an accredited ECI, an electronic signature does **not have the same legal validity** as a handwritten signature (Art. 14 LCE).

This matters when signing:

- Electronic invoices for the **SRI** (Ecuador's tax authority).
- **ECUAPASS / SENAE** customs declarations.
- Documents in **Quipux** (government).
- Private contracts that must be admissible **in court**.
- Filings at the **Civil Registry**, electronic notaries, and the **Judiciary**.

## How to choose your ECI

- **SRI invoices**: any ECI that issues `.p12`. Most popular among Ecuadorian accountants: Security Data, UANATACA, Eclipsoft, BCE.
- **Quipux (public sector)**: USB token usually required. Best fit: public ECIs (BCE, Civil Registry, ICERT-EC).
- **Sign from mobile / web**: any ECI that issues `.p12` directly (Security Data, UANATACA, ANF AC, ArgosData, Eclipsoft, Datil).
- **Cloud signature** (no `.p12` file to manage): UANATACA, ANF AC, Lazzate.
- **Get it fastest**: ECIs with remote video identification (ArgosData, UANATACA, Datil, ANF AC) — issuance in 24–72 business hours.
- **Compare published prices**: ICERT-EC lists **USD 27.60 including VAT for two years** for its `.p12` file certificate, checked on 16 September 2026. Compare the same format, validity period and tax basis; this figure alone does not establish which provider is cheapest. See [prices with sources](/en/pricing/).
